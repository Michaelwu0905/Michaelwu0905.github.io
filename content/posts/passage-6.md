## 范文
The diagrams illustrate how glass containers, such as bottles, are produced and recycled.

Overall, the production of glass containers consists of four main stages, starting with the mixing of raw materials and ending with the shaping of molten glass. The recycling process is slightly longer, involving five stages from collection of used glass to the moulding of new products.

In the production process, raw materials, including sand, soda ash, limestone and other chemicals, are first mixed together. The mixture is then placed in a glass furnace, where it is heated at approximately 1500 degrees Celsius until it melts. After this, the molten glass is shaped by blowing, and the final products are glass containers.

The recycling process begins with the collection of used glass. The collected glass is then sorted by color before being washed to remove impurities. In the next stage, it is crushed and melted, turning it back into molten glass. Finally, this material is moulded into new products.
